About The Blaque Hotel & Bistro

Serving exquisite food, comforting beverages, and genuine warmth 24 hours a day in the heart of Kilimani, Nairobi.

Our Culinary Philosophy

Where Passion Meets 24-Hour Convenience

Founded to provide Nairobi with exceptional hospitality that never clocks out, The Blaque Hotel & 24/7 Bistro has grown into a favored gathering spot for hotel guests, business travelers, and night owls across the city.

Whether you arrive at 3:00 AM on a long-distance flight or are hosting an early morning breakfast conference, our culinary team is standing by. We believe every customer deserves hot, freshly prepared meals made from whole ingredients—not reheated shortcuts.

Quality & Hygiene Standards

Food Preparation Round the Clock

Operating a 24-hour kitchen requires unyielding commitment to cleanliness, ingredient rotation, and culinary craft.

Certified Food Safety

Our kitchen adheres strictly to HACCP and Kenyan Ministry of Health public hygiene regulations. Food stations are sterilized across every 8-hour shift change.

Farm-Fresh Ingredients

We partner directly with reputable Kenyan poultry and dairy farmers, Naivasha vegetable growers, and Lake Victoria fisheries for daily fresh deliveries.

Dedicated Night Brigade

Our nocturnal culinary crew consists of fully qualified chefs and baristas trained to maintain day-time presentation, speed, and flavor excellence at 3 AM.

No Preservatives

From our freshly pressed juices to our slow-simmered beef stew gravy and authentic pilau spice blends, we cook with pure culinary passion.

Atmosphere & Spaces

The Garden Terrace

24/7 Barista Lounge
